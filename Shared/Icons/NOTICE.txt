These files are baby, jar, drop, drop-half, bowl-food, moon, pill, ruler, and spiral.
They are drawn locally. The app does not download them.

Wet uses drop. Dirty uses spiral. A diaper change uses drop-half.
Phosphor has no diaper glyph, so those three stay distinct without copying another app's icon.
